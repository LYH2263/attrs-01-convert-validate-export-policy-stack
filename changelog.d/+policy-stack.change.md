Added `attrs.policies` (also available as `attr.policies`), an instance-scoped, thread-local policy stack with per-frame `ConvertGate`, `ValidateGate` and `ExportProfile`. Frames temporarily force or skip converters/validators independently of each other, of field definitions and of the global `validators.set_disabled` switch, and let `asdict`/`astuple` export public-only or shallow, without changing the pinned behavior when the stack is empty. Frames propagate to construction during `evolve`; frozen classes, `setters.NO_OP` and all existing keyword arguments are unchanged.
